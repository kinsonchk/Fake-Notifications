How the valley unfolds

Tap a stage to jump there. Thresholds are mockup values.

    • Clean time counts minutes without a catch while monitoring is on (paused time doesn't count).
    • Reveal is the camera and mist only: health still controls grass height, hill colour (dry when low) and river level.
    • Landmarks (path, bridge, cottage, windmill) grow in when their stage is reached.
    • A catch rolls the mist back: one stage, or to the start, depending on the rule picked above.
    • Touch is play only: brushing bends grass, never changes clean time or health.
